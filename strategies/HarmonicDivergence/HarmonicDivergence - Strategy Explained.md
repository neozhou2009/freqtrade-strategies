# HarmonicDivergence Strategy: The Divergence Detective

> **Nickname**: Divergence Diva  
> **Timeframe**: 15 Minutes

---

## 1. What's This Strategy About?

**HarmonicDivergence** is a "divergence expert" strategy. It checks a whopping twelve indicators for divergences: price makes a new low but the indicators don't (bullish divergence) → BUY!

**Plain English Analogy**:
> Like watching runners:
> - Everyone's exhausted (price at new low)
> - But this one is still hanging in (indicator NOT at new low)
> - That means stamina is good! → Bottom pick! 🏃‍♂️

---

## 2. Core Settings

```
Take-profit: 1-5% (staggered)
Stop-loss: -10%
Trailing stop: 0.7% activation
```

---

## 3. What Indicators Does It Check?

The strategy checks **11 indicators** for divergences:
- RSI, Stochastic, ROC
- Ultimate Oscillator, Awesome Oscillator
- MACD, CCI
- CMF, OBV, MFI
- ADX

Plus Keltner Channel and Bollinger Bands for filtering!

---

## 4. Entry Conditions

**Bullish Divergence**:
- Price makes a new low
- But at least one of those 11 indicators did NOT make a new low
- Price breaks through Keltner Channel
