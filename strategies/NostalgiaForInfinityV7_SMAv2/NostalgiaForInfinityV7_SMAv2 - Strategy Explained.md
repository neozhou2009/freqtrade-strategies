## 3. Protection: Safe Dips + Safe Pump + SMA200

**Safe Dips**: Don't buy when the drop is too big. Has 11 levels — from super strict (Level 10) to super loose (Level 110).

**Safe Pump**: Don't buy when the price just surged. Checks 24h/36h/48h windows.

**SMA200**: The "magic line." If it's rising, the long-term trend is healthy. If it's falling, be cautious.

---

## 4. Sell Logic: 12-Level Profit-Taking + Trailing

**12 levels**: More profit → more relaxed on RSI (let it run). Less profit → stricter on RSI (protect principal).

**Trailing**: Three sets based on profit level and drawdown.

**Recovery**: Was down big, now back to profit? Take it and run!
